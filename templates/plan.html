✅ How to pick a good hotel
  • Look for a rating of 4.0+ on Google (or 8+ on Booking.com).
  • Prefer hotels with 100+ reviews. A few reviews can be fake or biased.
  • Read the newest reviews (last 3 months), not just the top ones.
  • Check guest photos, not only the hotel's own photos.
  • Check the distance to the main spot you are visiting.
  • Call the hotel and confirm the price and your dates before paying any advance.
